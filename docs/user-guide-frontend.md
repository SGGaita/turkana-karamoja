## Overview

Karamoja Climate Change Knowledge and Information is a public-facing web platform that delivers early warnings, climate reports, community services, and partner information to pastoral and agropastoral communities across Turkana and North Pokot in Kenya and Moroto, Amudat and Napak in Uganda.

The website is free to use and works on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile phones. No account is required to browse warnings, reports, or community content.

---

## Site Navigation

The main navigation bar provides access to all public sections of Karamoja:

| Page | Route | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Home | `/` | Dashboard with map, active warnings, initiatives, and quick links |
| About the Hub | `/about` | Mission, geographic coverage, and platform features |
| Early Warnings | `/early-warnings` | Live advisories sorted by severity (RED, ORANGE, YELLOW, GREEN) |
| Community | `/community` | Programmes, bulletins, water points, and community initiatives |
| Reports | `/reports` | Searchable library of situation reports and documents |
| Contact Us | `/contact` | Contact form and office directory |
| Partners & Stakeholders | `/partners-stakeholders` | Partner portal for registered organisations |
| Organizations | `/organizations` | Directory of verified partner organisations |

> **Note:** The top bar (above the main navigation) includes language selection, Login/Register, API Access, and Help links.

## Using Early Warnings

Early Warnings is the core alert system of the Climate Hub. Advisories are colour-coded by severity and updated as events develop.

### Severity Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **RED** | Severe or extreme impact; immediate action required |
| **ORANGE** | High risk; prepare and monitor closely |
| **YELLOW** | Moderate risk or watch condition |
| **GREEN** | Normal conditions or informational update |

### How to Use Early Warnings

1. Open **Early Warnings** from the main navigation or click the **Alerts** button in the header.
2. Browse the list of active advisories — the most severe alerts appear first.
3. Click any advisory to read the full text, recommended actions, affected area, and issuing source.
4. Use the map on the homepage to see geographic coverage of active warnings.

## Browsing Reports & Documents

1. Navigate to **Reports** from the main menu.
2. Use the search box to find reports by title, keyword, or organisation name.
3. Filter by category (food security, water, health, etc.) if filters are available.
4. Click a report card to open the full detail page with download links.
5. Preview PDF documents in the built-in viewer where supported.

> **Note:** Reports are published by verified partner organisations and reviewed by platform administrators before going live.

## Community Services

The Community page aggregates programmes, bulletins, water point status, humanitarian assistance locations, and community radio information relevant to the Turkana–Karamoja border region.

### Available Sections

- **Programmes** — Ongoing climate and resilience initiatives by partner organisations
- **Bulletins** — News and updates from the humanitarian and climate community
- **Community Initiatives** — Grassroots projects and local activities
- **Water Points** — Status of boreholes, pans, dams, and water trucking locations
- **Radio Broadcasts** — Daily advisory schedules on Turkana FM, Radio Karamoja, and other stations

---

## Organization Directory

1. Visit **Organizations** from the navigation or About page links.
2. Browse verified partner organisations contributing advisories and reports.
3. Click an organisation to view their profile, published advisories, and document library.

## Contact & Support

1. Go to **Contact Us** to send a message through the contact form.
2. Fill in your name, email, organisation (optional), subject, and message.
3. Submit the form — your enquiry is sent to the platform coordination team.
4. For emergencies, use the toll-free hotline **1192 (Kenya)** rather than the web form.

## Mobile Access & Offline Mode

- The site is fully responsive — use any modern browser on your phone or tablet.
- Add the site to your home screen for quick access (browser menu → **Add to Home Screen**).
- The platform supports Progressive Web App (PWA) features including offline cached content when connectivity is limited.
- An offline indicator appears at the bottom of the screen when you lose connection.

## Languages

The web platform is currently displayed in English. Community radio broadcasts and SMS alerts are available in Turkana, Ngakarimojong, Swahili, and English. Multi-language web support is planned for a future release.

The language selector in the top bar shows available languages; full switching will be enabled in a future update.

## Partner Portal (Organisations)

If you represent a humanitarian, government, or research organisation, visit **Partners & Stakeholders** to register and submit advisories or reports. See the [Backend & Admin Guide](./user-guide-backend.md) for the full registration and submission workflow.

### Getting Started as a Partner

1. Click **Login / Register** in the top bar or **Partners & Stakeholders** in the main nav.
2. Register your organisation with contact details and organisation type.
3. Wait for administrator approval — you will receive an email to set your password.
4. Sign in and use the dashboard to submit advisories, reports, and track submission status.
